Everyone who asks me about Play-to-Earn wants to know which game pays the most. I understand the question, but it's the wrong one. The right question is: which game would you play if it paid nothing?

The P2E landscape has changed a lot since the gold rush years. The games that were pure earning mechanisms — grind this, sell that, extract value and leave — are mostly gone or hollowed out. What survived, what's actually worth your time in 2026, are the games that were good games first and economies second.

> Start with the game. The economy is secondary. It was always secondary.

SuperWalk works for me because I was already walking. The WALK tokens are a nice bonus, but the real value is that the app gave me a frame for something I already wanted to do. It turned a daily habit into something with a small visible record. The NFT is a tool for that, not a lottery ticket.

Sunflower Land works for people who genuinely like the idle farming loop. It has a real economy with real complexity — but the people who burn out fast are the ones who came for the economy and tolerated the game. The people who stick around came for the game and treat the economy as an interesting layer on top.

The practical advice, then, is boring:

Start small. Pick one game you'd play for free. Give it a month before you spend anything. Watch how you feel about it on a Tuesday afternoon when the numbers aren't moving. If you still want to play it — that's your answer.

Everything else follows from that.
